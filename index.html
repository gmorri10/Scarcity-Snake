High supply — fruit is plentiful.

Click the board, then use the arrow keys to move. The board begins with lots of fruit. Every time you consume fruit, its replacement takes longer, making supply increasingly scarce.

Economics idea Demand (you) consumes the available supply of fruit. As consumption increases, the replenishment lag grows, so scarcity becomes more noticeable and finding the next available fruit gets harder.
